Hello, I’m Hong.

Designer, writer & photographer based in Singapore. A curiosity that has stayed with me.

More than a decade ago, I wrote technology columns for magazines and newspapers. I stopped writing those columns, but I never stopped buying things and trying them out.

I change phones four or five times a year, and have ten cameras and three 3D printers at home. I also enjoy appliances and all sorts of interesting little things. I spend time with them, pass them on, and try something else.

Now I want to write down what these past few years have left me with. There is room for reflection, and for stories that begin with a small detail.

Finding M is my personal site: notes on objects, and on the life around them. I am based in Singapore.
