PhotoPro Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Developer: Zhu Hong · Contact: zivzhu@gmail.com

1. Processing photographs

PhotoPro browses, selects and exports photographs on macOS. It reads photos and EXIF from folders you select and processes previews, filters, crops and exports on your Mac. The app does not collect or upload your photographs, EXIF, locations, file names, paths, ratings or favorites to the developer. We do not operate a photo server.

2. Local storage

Sources, folder-access bookmarks, ratings, source-to-favorite mappings and interface preferences are stored locally. Preview images are cached in memory. Crops last for the current app session and filters reset when navigating. Favoriting creates a copy; exporting creates a new PNG.

3. Permissions and network disks

You grant folder access through the system picker. The Mac App Store build uses App Sandbox and security-scoped bookmarks to restore that access. PhotoPro does not request access to your entire system Photos library, camera, microphone, contacts or location. A NAS transfers files between your Mac and the server you choose; macOS/Finder handles authentication. PhotoPro does not store server passwords. Server operators may maintain access logs under their own settings and policies.

4. Advertising and tracking

The app has no advertisements, analytics SDKs, tracking identifiers or developer-operated crash uploads. No account or in-app purchase is required. Apple may process store transactions and diagnostic or usage information according to your system settings and Apple’s privacy policy; this is separate from PhotoPro’s data practices.

5. Retention, deletion and control

Removing a source removes its library entry and saved bookmark, not the photographs. About PhotoPro → Usage & Support → Reset Local Library Records clears sources, saved permissions, ratings, favorite mappings, crops and preferences. Originals, favorite copies and exports remain for you to manage in Finder. In-flight reads may complete; open access scopes are released when the app quits. There is no cloud account or developer-held account record to delete. Deleting a photograph moves it to Trash, or to a nearby _PhotoPro_Deleted folder when Trash is unavailable. Undo is available during the app session, and files can also be restored in Finder. Unfavoriting only moves the mapped copy. Favorite copies, exports and server backups may contain original or identifying information; check them before sharing or deleting.

6. Support and the website

If you choose to email support, the developer receives your email address, message and any attachments you select. Avoid sending unnecessary private photographs or sensitive information. Messages are used to respond and diagnose problems and deleted when no longer needed, except where retention is legally required; email providers may keep backups under their policies. You may request deletion by email. The app’s no-collection statement does not cover website hosting logs. Website hosting may process IP addresses, request paths, browser information and request times. PhotoPro pages set no analytics or advertising cookies and embed no third-party tracking scripts. Your email provider’s policy applies when sending mail.

7. Updates and contact

If practices change, we will update this policy and applicable App Store privacy disclosures. The current policy is at https://zhuhong.me/photopro/privacy.html. Contact Zhu Hong at zivzhu@gmail.com with privacy questions.

PhotoPro 隐私声明

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 8 日
开发者：Zhu Hong
联系邮箱：zivzhu@gmail.com

1. 照片在你选择的位置处理

PhotoPro 是一款 macOS 照片浏览、筛选和导出工具。应用读取你选择的文件夹中的照片和 EXIF 信息，在本机生成预览、应用滤镜、裁切和导出。我们不通过应用收集或上传照片、EXIF、位置、文件名、文件路径、星级或收藏信息，不建立开发者可访问的照片服务器。

2. 本地保存的内容

应用在本机保存来源文件夹、文件夹访问书签、星级、原图与收藏副本的对应关系及界面偏好。图像预览缓存在内存中。裁切范围只在当前应用会话中保存，退出后清除；滤镜切换照片后复位。收藏会创建一份照片副本，导出会创建新的 PNG 文件。

3. 文件权限与网络磁盘

你通过系统文件选择器授权文件夹访问。Mac App Store 版本使用 App Sandbox 和安全作用域书签恢复授权。应用不请求整个系统照片图库、相机、麦克风、通讯录或定位权限。选择 NAS 时，文件会在你的 Mac 与你选择的网络服务器之间传输；连接和认证由 macOS/Finder 处理，PhotoPro 不保存 NAS 密码。网络服务器及其管理者可能保存访问日志，其数据处理由该服务器的设置和政策决定。

4. 无广告与追踪

应用不包含广告、行为分析 SDK、追踪标识符或开发者自行上传的崩溃日志。无需注册账号，也没有应用内购买。Apple 可能依照你的系统设置处理商店交易、诊断或使用信息，适用 Apple 的隐私政策；这不等同于 PhotoPro 自行收集上述数据。

5. 删除、保留与控制

移除来源只移除图库入口和保存的访问书签，不会删除照片。你可以在“关于 PhotoPro”的“使用与支持”中重置本机图库记录，清除来源、书签、星级、收藏对应关系、裁切和偏好；原图、收藏副本及导出文件保留，由你在 Finder 中管理。已开始的文件读取可能完成，已打开的授权作用域在退出应用后释放。应用没有云端账号，因此没有开发者保存的账号数据可供删除。
删除照片会优先移至系统废纸篓；不能使用废纸篓时，移至照片旁的 _PhotoPro_Deleted 文件夹。你可以在本次会话中撤销，或在 Finder 中恢复。取消收藏只移动与该原图对应的收藏副本。收藏副本、导出图片及网络服务器的备份可能保留原始照片或可识别的信息，请在分享和清理前检查。

6. 联系支持及网站

只有当你主动发送支持邮件时，开发者才会收到你的邮箱、消息内容和你选择附上的文件。请勿发送不必要的私人照片或敏感信息。支持邮件用于答复和排查问题，在不再需要处理问题时删除，法律要求保留的情况除外；邮件服务商可能依照其政策保留备份。你可通过上述邮箱要求删除支持往来。
本声明中的“不收集”描述应用本身。打开网站时，网站托管服务可能处理 IP 地址、请求路径、浏览器信息和时间等访问日志。PhotoPro 配套网页不设置分析或广告 Cookie，也不嵌入第三方追踪脚本。发送邮件适用你所使用的邮件服务的政策。

7. 更新与联系

数据处理方式变化时，我们会更新本声明及适用的 App Store 隐私信息。最新声明：https://zhuhong.me/photopro/privacy.html。有关隐私的问题可联系 Zhu Hong：zivzhu@gmail.com。
