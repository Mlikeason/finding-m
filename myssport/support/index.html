Using Myssport

How do I add a member?

Tap Add Member, enter at least one name, then Save. Tap the member’s name to expand the card. Add passport details, personal information or visas from there. Use the member options menu to rename or delete the member.

How do expiry indicators work?

The expiry overview includes expired records and records expiring within 365 days. Under 90 days is critical; 90–364 days is expiring. The displayed expiry day is included as the last valid day for this date indicator. Myssport does not schedule notifications or verify actual document validity, visa conditions or entry requirements.

How do I export or restore a backup?

Use Settings → Export Backup. The JSON file is not encrypted; store it only in a location you trust. Restore Backup validates a versioned Myssport backup and asks before replacing all existing records. Export the current records first if you need to keep them. Your PIN is not included in the file. The prototype’s older clipboard export cannot be restored using this feature.

What if I forget my PIN?

PINs cannot be recovered. On the lock screen, select Forgot PIN and follow the erasure confirmation. This removes all local records and the PIN; restore a backup afterwards if you have one. Deleting and reinstalling the app may retain the PIN in Keychain. Incorrect attempts are limited with a waiting period after five failures.

How can I remove my records?

Delete a member from their options menu, delete a visa from its editor, or use Settings → Delete All Records. Member deletion also removes related passport, contact and visa details. Separately remove any exported files or device backups you no longer want.

Does Myssport sync or support Apple Wallet?

Version 1.0 stores records locally and supports manual backup and restore. It has no automatic cloud sync or Apple Wallet integration. A record in Myssport is not an official digital travel document.

Why were unsaved changes lost?

When PIN protection is enabled, moving the app to the background closes access to your records and may discard unsaved forms. Save before switching apps.

Why can’t my records open or save?

Unlock your device, check available storage, and retry. A failed save reports an error; it should not report success. Avoid deleting the app unless you have a verified backup. Contact support with the error and reproduction steps, without sending your records.
