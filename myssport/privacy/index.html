Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 · Version 1.0 · Provider: Zhu Hong

Myssport is an offline passport and visa organiser. This policy explains the records you enter, the protection provided by the app, and the choices you control.

Records stored on your device

The app stores names, passport details, dates of birth, identity numbers, contact details, account names and visa dates you enter in a local database. Myssport does not create an online account or send these records to Zhu Hong or a developer-operated server.

The app contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs. Your records are not sold, used for advertising or shared automatically.

Device protection and app PIN

The local database uses iOS file protection. Use a device passcode and enable the optional app PIN. The PIN is stored as a salted, slow-derived value in the device Keychain. App access locks in the background, and the app covers its content while inactive. Neither feature prevents someone from reading a file you export or taking a screenshot while the app is unlocked.

The app does not offer its own cloud sync. iOS device backups may include your records depending on your system backup settings. The app PIN is stored only in this device’s Keychain and may remain after an app reinstall.

Clipboard and exported files

Values you copy are marked for this device only and expire from the system clipboard after 60 seconds. When you paste into another app, that app receives the value.

Exported JSON backups are readable, unencrypted files. You choose the destination through the system file picker; it may be a local folder or a cloud provider. The destination’s own terms and privacy practices apply. Anyone with access to the file can read the records. Keep backups private and remove copies you no longer need.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete members and visas at any time. Deleting a member also removes that member’s passport, personal details and visas. Settings → Delete All Records removes all records from the app. Forgotten-PIN recovery erases the local records and PIN after confirmation.

Deleting records does not delete previously exported files, files shared with others or older device backups. You control those separately. Uninstalling removes the app container, but may not remove its Keychain PIN. Zhu Hong cannot recover records or PINs.

Optional support correspondence

If you contact zivzhu@gmail.com, your email address and the information you choose to provide are used to respond to your support request. Correspondence is handled through the email service and may be retained while needed to resolve the request. You may request deletion of that correspondence by email, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Do not send identity documents, passport numbers, PINs, passwords or backup files to support.

Family information

Only store information you are authorised to manage. A parent or guardian should manage any records relating to children. The app does not provide services or advertising directed at children.

This website

These support pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or cookies set by Myssport. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site. Other pages on zhuhong.me may have separate practices.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app’s data handling changes. The effective date appears above. For privacy questions, contact Zhu Hong at zivzhu@gmail.com.
